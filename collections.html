Explore the Collections

Explore the pieces featured in this project by how they can be used: for tea and coffee, for serving a meal, or as decorative accessories. These are editorial groups, rather than official collection names. For individual items and recorded prices, visit the product catalogue.

  • Tea and coffee
  • Table setting
  • Decorative accessories

Tea and Coffee

A cup, a matching saucer, and a teapot provide a starting point for a tea or coffee setting. The existing catalogue features a blue-patterned mug and a matching plate. Compare the shapes and decoration when choosing pieces to use together.

Think about whether you need one cup for yourself or several places for guests. Ask the store which matching pieces are currently available and whether they are sold separately.

  • Mugs and cups for individual drinks
  • Saucers to accompany cups
  • Teapots for serving at the table
A mug and plate already featured in the project catalogue.

Table Setting

A table setting brings together pieces used for eating and serving. The project catalogue includes bowls, dishes, pitchers, and decanters. Start with the number of people you want to serve, then consider which shared serving pieces would be useful.

Use the display photograph to explore how different pieces can be arranged together. Check the contents of a set with the store before choosing it: a display does not necessarily show everything included in a single purchase.

  • Bowls and dishes for food
  • Pitchers and decanters for serving drinks
  • Shared serving pieces for the centre of the table
A tableware display photographed for the existing project.

Decorative Accessories

The catalogue also records candle holders and jewellery boxes. These smaller objects offer another way to explore the store alongside its tableware. Consider where a piece will be placed and how its size and decoration will fit the surrounding space.

Before selecting an accessory as a gift, ask about its dimensions, care instructions, and packaging. Confirm those details for the specific item instead of assuming that all products have the same materials or handling requirements.

See the catalogue categories for the product groups already documented in this project.

Before Choosing a Piece

  1. Decide how you want to use it and how many pieces you need.
  2. Compare the items and recorded prices in the catalogue.
  3. Contact the store to confirm availability and current details.
