Featured Product

Mug

I really liked the standard-shaped white mug with a beautiful design depicting Paris—specifically, the Eiffel Tower in shades of blue. It costs ₸120,000 and has the original name Tout Parts Bleu by Bernardaud. It looks expensive, elegant, and comfortable to use for tea and coffee. I liked its design and how uniquely the image is painted on the mug. It’s made of porcelain, which is why it’s highly valued—and fragile. It feels nice to hold and is very light; in addition to the Eiffel Tower, there are birds, trees, and, basically, the natural scenery of Paris. It’s sold as part of a set with plates, but you can also buy it separately in a gift box. Compared to the others, it doesn’t really stand out, but that’s exactly why I liked it. I’d buy this for myself or my mom if I felt like treating myself to something. The consultants are friendly and helpful when choosing, and it’s clear they are professionals who know their stuff.
