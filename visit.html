Visit the Store

A visit gives you an opportunity to look at the tableware in person and ask questions about individual pieces. Before travelling, browse the catalogue and note the items that interest you. The photographs and prices in this project document an earlier visit and do not guarantee current availability.

If you need parking information, step-free access, or public transport directions, ask the store for current details. These facilities and routes have not been confirmed in this project.

Before You Go

  1. Browse the collections and note the pieces you want to see.
  2. Decide how many place settings or individual items you need.
  3. Measure the available storage space if you are considering a larger set.
  4. Call the store to check opening hours and availability.

For a present, bring a few notes about the recipient's preferences and your budget. The gift guide can help you organise those questions before arriving.

Questions During Your Visit

Ask about the exact piece you are considering. Items that look similar may differ in dimensions, decoration, or intended use. Compare a complete set with individual pieces before deciding which option fits your needs.

  • Which pieces are included in the displayed set?
  • Are matching items available separately?
  • What are the material and care instructions?
  • What packaging and purchase conditions apply to this item?

Read Care and Storage for more questions to ask about cleaning and everyday use.
