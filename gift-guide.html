Gift Guide

A useful gift starts with the person receiving it. Think about their daily habits, the space they have, and the pieces they already enjoy. This guide groups items documented in the Noble catalogue by possible occasions. Confirm current stock, prices, and packaging with the store before making a purchase.

Browse the ideas below or jump to the practice gift enquiry form.

For a New Home

Start by finding out what the recipient still needs. A bowl or pitcher from the catalogue could complement their existing tableware. A single useful piece may be easier to accommodate than a large set, especially when storage space is limited.

  • Check the colours and shapes of their existing pieces.
  • Ask about the dimensions and intended use of the item.
  • Confirm which pieces are included if choosing a set.

For Someone Who Enjoys Tea or Coffee

The catalogue features a patterned mug, saucers, and a teapot. Consider whether the recipient usually drinks alone or serves guests. Their preferred cup size and the comfort of the handle can matter as much as the decoration.

Explore the tea and coffee section to compare the types of pieces featured in the project.

For a Wedding or Special Celebration

Ask what the recipients would actually use before choosing a serving piece. The catalogue includes dishes and decanters, while candle holders and jewellery boxes offer decorative alternatives. Choose around the recipients' preferences rather than assuming that a larger or more expensive gift will suit them better.

Ask the store whether suitable gift packaging is available for the selected item. This page does not confirm wrapping, delivery, engraving, or any other additional service.

Plan Your Choice

  1. Choose an occasion and note the recipient's preferences.
  2. Set a budget and decide how many gifts you need.
  3. Compare the recorded catalogue prices.
  4. Confirm availability, care instructions, and packaging with the store.
